How the farm works

We treat every crop line as a production system with its own cycle, inputs and buyers. That keeps quality steady and waste low.

Grow

Greenhouse racks for microgreens on 7–14 day cycles, a humidity-controlled room for mushrooms, and outdoor beds for saffron, herbs and garlic.

Measure

Soil and pH testing by zone, yield logs per harvest, and a shared task list the whole team works from.

Sell direct

Restaurants, wellness shops and CSA members order straight from this site. No middle layer.

Connect

Saffron origins, research centres and standards bodies on one map, plus every grower and buyer who joins.
