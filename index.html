🌸 Saffron — The Red Gold

Saffron is the most expensive spice in the world by weight. A single acre can yield 2–5 lbs of dried saffron annually. Each pound sells for $5,000–$10,000 to specialty buyers, restaurants, and online.

Harvest: Oct–Nov
Climate: Dry summers
Water: Drought tolerant
Market: Restaurants, online
Bulbs (corms) — 0.5 acres–$3,000
Soil prep & irrigation–$1,500
Labor (harvest is manual)–$2,000
Expected yield (2.5 lbs)+$18,750
Net Year 1+$12,250
🌱 Microgreens — Fast Cash Crops

Microgreens grow in 7–14 days and sell for $25–$50 per flat or $25–$40/lb to restaurants and farmers markets. A 1,000 sq ft greenhouse can generate $50,000–$100,000/year running continuous cycles.

Cycle: 7–14 days
Market: Restaurants, direct
Space: Indoor/greenhouse
Top varieties: sunflower, pea, radish
Greenhouse setup–$5,000
Seeds & growing media–$2,000/yr
Lighting & water–$3,600/yr

Net Year 1+$21,400
🍄 Gourmet Mushrooms

Oyster, shiitake, and lion's mane mushrooms sell for $6–$20/lb wholesale and $15–$40/lb retail. They grow year-round indoors on inexpensive substrates like straw or wood chips.

Market: Restaurants, health food

12-Month Farm Roadmap
Month 1
Foundation & Setup
Soil testing, irrigation install, greenhouse frame. Obtain permits. Order all seeds & spawn.
Month 2
First Microgreens Harvest
First tray planted week 1, first revenue by week 2. Begin selling to local restaurants.
Month 3
Mushrooms Fruiting + Herbs Planted
Oyster mushrooms begin fruiting. Lavender, echinacea, ashwagandha seedlings in ground.
Month 4–5
Garlic Planted + Scale Microgreens
Specialty garlic cloves in ground (plant Sep–Oct). Microgreens at 3–4 concurrent trays.
Month 6–7
Plant Saffron Corms
Plant 0.5 acre of saffron corms Aug–Sep for Oct–Nov bloom. CSA subscriptions launch.
Month 8–9
🌸 Saffron Harvest (BIG REVENUE)
Flowers bloom at dawn. Hand-harvest stigmas daily. Dry and package. First saffron sales.
Month 10–11
🧄 Garlic Harvest + Herb Drying
Garlic pulled, cured 3 weeks. First herb harvest — lavender bundles, dried ashwagandha root.
Month 12
Year-End Review + Scale Plan
Assess yields. Plan Year 2 expansion. Reinvest in more saffron corms (self-propagating).
Where to Sell
Local restaurants & chefs
Highest price, consistent orders. Start cold-calling with samples.
Farmers markets

Online (Etsy, own website)
Dried saffron, herbs, garlic ship well. 2x–3x wholesale price.
CSA subscriptions
Weekly boxes — guaranteed recurring income. $30–$50/box.
Wellness shops & herbalists
Dried medicinal herbs wholesale in bulk.
Risks & Mitigation
Weather / frost
Use row covers, cold frames. Diversify crops.
Market access
Line up 3+ buyers before harvest. Don't rely on one.
Pest & disease
IPM approach. Keep mushroom area sterile. Rotate herbs.
Labor at saffron harvest
Hire temp help 3–4 weeks. Budget $2K for harvest labor.
Year 1 saffron yield low
Normal — corms multiply x3 in 3 years. Other crops cover cash flow.
